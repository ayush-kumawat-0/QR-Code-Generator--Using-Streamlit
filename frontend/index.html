From details to download

Create your QR in four simple steps.

Start with the information you want to share. QR Studio builds the right payload for the type you select, then sends it to the Python generator.

01

Choose a QR type

Select Website, WhatsApp, Email, SMS, Wi-Fi, Contact, or Calendar. The form changes to match your choice.

02

Enter the details

Fill in the required fields, such as a full website URL, message, Wi-Fi credentials, contact details, or event information.

03

Customize the look

Choose a foreground and background with strong contrast, pick a dot style, and add a small logo if needed.

04

Generate and check

Press Generate QR, check the preview, then download or copy the PNG. Scan it with a phone before sharing or printing.

A few helpful answers

Questions, answered.

Quick guidance for creating, customizing, and sharing your QR codes.

Want to make one now?

Choose the content type first. The generator will show the fields you need to complete.

Open the QR generator →
What kinds of QR codes can I create?

QR Studio supports Website, WhatsApp, Email, SMS, Wi-Fi, Contact, and Calendar QR codes. Choose a type in the generator to see its matching fields.

How do I get the best result?

Complete all required fields, use a clear foreground/background contrast (usually dark modules on a light background), keep any centre logo small, and scan the downloaded PNG with a phone before printing or sharing it.

Can I change the colors and dot style?

Yes. Open “Customize appearance” to set the QR foreground and background colors, then choose Square, Rounded, or Soft for the module style.

Can I add my own logo to the centre?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPEG, or WebP image in the Center logo control. Keep important details away from the centre and test the resulting QR code with a phone camera.

Where is my QR code generated?

The interface sends the selected content and appearance settings to this application's Python QR generator. It does not rely on a third-party QR-generation API.

Where can I find my recent QR codes?

Open “Recent” in the top navigation. The recent payload history is stored in this browser's local storage; clearing browser site data or using another browser/device won't carry that history over.

Why won't my QR code scan?

Try a darker foreground, a lighter background, a smaller logo, and a fresh PNG download. Avoid very similar colors and always test the final image at the size where it will be used.

Can I edit a QR code after downloading it?

The downloaded image represents the content you encoded when you generated it. To change the destination or message, return to the generator, update the fields, and generate a new QR code.
